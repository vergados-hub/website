Air Cargo Challenge
Engineering for flight.

Selected work
Project notes

Context

The Air Cargo Challenge brings student engineering teams together to design and operate aircraft around a constrained cargo-carrying mission. I contributed to the structural sub-team of the M.ACH project.

My involvement

The experience introduced me to the practical connection between engineering design, constraints, fabrication and team coordination in a competition environment.

  • Contributed to structural design and development activities for an air-cargo UAV.
  • Supported design/manufacturing work within a multidisciplinary student team.
  • Participated in a team that finished 13th among 30 competitors at the 2024 Air Cargo Challenge in Aachen.

The result is attributed to the full M.ACH team, not to an individual contribution.
