Project notes

Context

Entrepreneurship Talks is a youth-led nonprofit working across entrepreneurship education, media, community events and international projects. What began as an initiative around conversations and ecosystem access has developed into a platform for partnerships and European programmes.

My involvement

My involvement includes organisational direction, partnerships, EU programme development and the operational work required to grow an initiative beyond its original format.

  • Co-founded and helped develop the organisation, including activities connecting young people with founders and innovation ecosystems.
  • Worked on international partnerships, EU programme development and organisational coordination.
  • Supported the development of podcast, educational, networking and community initiatives.
  • Contributed to securing an Erasmus+ KA153-YOU mobility grant as coordinating organisation.
