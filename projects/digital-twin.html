Selected work
Project notes

Context

My diploma thesis focused on the design and development of a digital twin of a hybrid manufacturing cell, connecting robotic production processes with a digital environment for simulation and process-control exploration.

My involvement

The project combined mechanical engineering, manufacturing systems, automation and digital modelling. It was developed within the Mechanical & Aeronautical Engineering programme at the University of Patras.

  • Explored digital-twin architecture for an industrial cell featuring a six-axis robotic system.
  • Connected engineering modelling, process logic and digital representation of manufacturing operations.
  • Developed a stronger foundation in systems thinking, automation and the interface between software and machinery.

This page is a high-level academic project summary. Thesis figures and original renders can be incorporated with permission and proper attribution.
