Project notes

Context

The Adamów Combined Cycle Gas Turbine project is a 560 MW net power plant in Poland, delivered within an EPC framework by the METLEN–Siemens Energy consortium. The plant’s scale is project context; my role is a contribution within the wider engineering and construction team.

My involvement

My work is at the interfaces between engineering, technical procurement, subcontracting and construction. I support the translation of technical requirements into packages, documentation and coordination that teams can execute.

  • Review multidisciplinary engineering information, including P&IDs, layouts, mechanical specifications and piping documentation.
  • Prepare and coordinate RFQs and scopes of work for mechanical and construction work packages.
  • Support supplier and subcontractor clarifications, interface coordination and technical follow-up.
  • Participate in periodic site assignments involving constructability and engineering-to-field coordination.
