02 / About

Engineering, people
& perspective.

I’m a mechanical engineer with an interest in how complex projects come together — not only through design and technical decisions, but through organisations, people and execution.

I studied Mechanical & Aeronautical Engineering at the University of Patras, with a technical foundation in manufacturing, automation and mechanical systems. My diploma thesis explored a digital twin for a hybrid robotic manufacturing cell.

Today, I work as a Mechanical Project Engineer at METLEN, supporting the delivery of international EPC energy infrastructure. My responsibilities sit between engineering, procurement and construction: reviewing technical deliverables, helping define subcontractor packages, coordinating interfaces and learning from the realities of site execution.

Outside my engineering role, I co-founded Entrepreneurship Talks, a nonprofit initiative centred on entrepreneurship education, media, community and international opportunity. Building the organisation has involved partnerships, programmes, people and responsibilities very different from — but complementary to — engineering.

I’m interested in project delivery, industrial systems, entrepreneurship and the commercial side of building things. I value technical depth, clear thinking, and working with people who turn ideas into something tangible.
